KHEDUTMITRA - DATA SOURCE NOTES
=================================
Source: Agmarknet.gov.in (Government of India, Ministry of Agriculture)
Report type: All Type of Report (All Grades) - Market Wise, Date Wise
Date range: 01-Jan-2021 to 24-Sep-2026
Data fields: State, District, Market, Commodity Group, Commodity, Date, Arrival Quantity, Arrival Unit, Modal Price, Price Unit

CROP-WISE DATA COLLECTED:
1. Groundnut   - Rajkot district        - file: groundnut_rajkot_district_2021_2026.csv
2. Cotton      - Surendranagar district - file: cotton_surendranagar_district_2021_2026.csv
3. Sesame      - Patan district         - file: sesame_patan_district_2021_2026.csv
4. Wheat       - Sabarkantha district   - file: wheat_sabarkantha_district_2021_2026.csv
5. Pearl Millet (Bajra) - Patan district - file: bajra_patan_district_2021_2026.csv
6. Castor Seed - Banaskantha district   - file: castor_banaskantha_district_2021_2026.csv
7. Chickpea    - Botad district         - file: chickpea_botad_district_2021_2026.csv
8. Cumin       - Mehsana district       - file: cumin_mehsana_district_2021_2026.csv
9. Mustard (replaced Fennel) - Surendranagar district - file: mustard_surendranagar_district_2021_2026.csv

NOTE: Each district-level file contains multiple mandis/APMCs (typically 3-6+ 
markets per district) reporting for that commodity, not just one market.
The exact list of markets per crop is derived directly from the 'Market' 
column within each CSV file during data cleaning.

NOTE ON MUSTARD: Fennel seed was originally planned but was not available 
at mandi-level granularity for any tested Gujarat district on Agmarknet. 
Mustard was substituted as it is a genuinely traded Gujarat oilseed crop 
with available historical data.
